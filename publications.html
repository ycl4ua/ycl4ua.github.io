Publications First Author

IEEE ISBI 2025Oral

Self-supervised Graph Transformer with Contrastive Learning for Brain Connectivity Analysis towards Improving Autism Detection

Yicheng Leng, Syed Muhammad Anwar, Islem Rekik, Sen He, Eung-Joo Lee

A brain-network transformer pre-trained with contrastive learning over randomly altered connectivity graphs, reaching 82.6 AUROC and 74% accuracy on ABIDE autism detection.

Paper arXiv

SPIE Medical Imaging 2025

SLRBC: Self-supervised Learning for Red Blood Cell Segmentation and Classification with a Biased Contrastive Loss

Yicheng Leng, Eung-Joo Lee

Picks harder negatives by image similarity and pairs them with a biased contrastive loss, lifting red blood cell classification accuracy to 0.840 over SimCLR, MoCo, and DINO.

Paper

AAAI 2025

Bridging Knowledge Gap Between Image Inpainting and Large-Area Visible Watermark Removal

Yicheng Leng, Chaowei Fang, Junye Chen, Yixiang Fang, Sheng Li, Guanbin Li

Brings the prior of a pretrained inpainting model to large-area watermark removal by gated fusion of watermark-cleaned features, and trains on coarse masks so it holds up when the mask is imprecise.

Paper arXiv Code

AAAI 2024

Removing interference and recovering content imaginatively for visible watermark removal

Yicheng Leng, Chaowei Fang, Gen Li, Yixiang Fang, Guanbin Li

Separates watermark exclusion from background restoration, then fuses content recovered from beneath the watermark with content imagined from the surrounding context (HWVOC PSNR 39.52).
